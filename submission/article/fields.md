RescueFlow AI: Building an Agentic Disaster Response Decision System with Strands Decider 2B on AWS

Description (max 512 characters):
RescueFlow AI is an agentic emergency-response triage prototype that combines Amazon Bedrock with Strands Decider 2B. The agent collects incident facts through 8 tools, assembles a structured state, and calls Strands Decider 2B in one forward pass to classify each incident as allow, review, or BLOCK. A Decision Arena compares competing incidents live and re-evaluates when conditions change. Benchmark: 4.3x faster than Nova Pro at p50, 93.8% accuracy when confident.
